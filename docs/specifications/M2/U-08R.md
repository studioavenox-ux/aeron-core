## Reported historical descriptions and boundaries

- **U-8R.1 (`RECOVERED`, Owner-reported):** AERON and its agents operate autonomously within defined boundaries. Autonomy remains within permissions; original question and alternatives `UNRESOLVED`.
- **U-8R.2:** Shared Authority is a distinct confirmed selection; it does not remove the Owner's ultimate authority. Original alternatives `UNRESOLVED`.
- **U-8R.3:** MFA is confirmed; factor combination, technology, enrollment, fallback and recovery `UNRESOLVED`.
- **U-8R.4 (`RECOVERED`, Owner-reported):** encryption at rest; encryption of communications; key management; protected backups. Algorithms and key recovery `UNRESOLVED`.
- **U-8R.5:** Custom Security Architecture is confirmed; the custom privacy/security policy follow-up was left undefined and is `UNRESOLVED`. No custom policy is invented.
- **U-8R.6 (`RECOVERED`, Owner-reported):** threat detection, component isolation, shutdown, alerts, controlled recovery. The reported alternatives, preserved without extrapolation to other questions: A — Manual Shutdown; B — Automatic Threat Detection; C — Automatic Emergency Shutdown; D — Comprehensive Emergency Protection; E — Custom Emergency Architecture.
- **U-8R.7:** Comprehensive Audit System is confirmed. Audit coverage, integrity, access and recovery must be addressed; technical implementation is `PROPOSED`.
- **U-8R.8:** Controlled Intelligent Access is confirmed. Its implementation must be reconciled with default deny and Owner approvals; technical mapping `PROPOSED`.
- **U-8R.9 (`RECOVERED`, Owner-reported):** device identity and trust; multi-factor authentication; per-device permissions; risk-based authorization; logging; remote revocation.
- **U-8R.10:** Custom Recovery Architecture is confirmed; Google Drive is the recorded backup destination. Historical account context of 5 TB and approximately 1% used is Owner-reported at the time of discussion, not current verified availability. Recovery is separate from application backup scope.
- **U-8R.11 (`RECOVERED`, Owner-reported):** Complete Application Backup covers complete AERON application data; original full wording `UNRESOLVED`. Google Drive is the recorded destination, not an answer letter or unrestricted-upload authorization. Encryption, credentials, retention and restore procedures require separate review.
- **U-8R.12:** Component Isolation and User Alert is confirmed; Windows isolation mechanism `UNRESOLVED`.
- **U-8R.13:** Comprehensive Permission Revocation is confirmed; reconcile immediate revocation, propagation to agents, preventing subsequent unauthorized use and pre-execution checks. Mechanisms `PROPOSED`.
- **U-8R.14:** Comprehensive Security Notifications is confirmed; reconcile coverage, sensitivity, delivery and Owner controls; mechanisms `PROPOSED`.
- **U-8R.15 (`RECOVERED`, Owner-reported):** shared governance, bounded authority, no self-elevation of privileges. This is separate from U-8R.2 and the authority ADRs.

**`CONFIRMED`:** Sharing personal files or information with external services requires Owner approval. Google Drive does not authorize unrestricted uploads, disclosure, paid usage, or OmniRoute changes. G2/G4 and C-12 apply as relevant.

**`PROPOSED` safeguards, not separate historical Owner selections:** encryption before backup upload; separate key management; backup integrity verification; controlled restoration. Protected backups and key management are independently recorded under U-8R.4, but the specific safeguards here remain proposals.
